Commodity Loan Application

Commodity Loan Terms & Conditions

✅ Eligibility Requirements
  • Account Status: Your account must be in ACTIVE status. Pending or inactive accounts cannot apply for commodity loans.
  • Minimum Enrollment Period: You must complete 4 Months before you can apply for a commodity loan.
  • Share Amount Requirement: Your total share deduction amount must be at least ₹16,000 before you can apply for a commodity loan.
💰 Loan Amount & Limits
  • New Members: Fixed loan amount of ₹50,000 (as per system configuration).
    • This amount is predetermined and cannot be customized
  • Existing Members: Fixed loan amount of ₹1,00,000.
    • Higher limit for experienced members with established trading history
🚫 Restrictions & Important Conditions
  • Outstanding Loan Restriction: If you have any outstanding commodity loan, you cannot apply for a new commodity loan until the previous one is fully repaid.
  • Cannot Apply If:
    • Your account status is not ACTIVE
    • Your Society joining date is less than 4 months
    • Your share amount is below ₹16,000
    • You have an existing commodity loan with outstanding balance
    • Previous commodity loan application is in PENDING status
*
*marked are mandatory fields.
