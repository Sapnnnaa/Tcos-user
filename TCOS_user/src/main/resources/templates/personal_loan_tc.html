Personal Loan Application

💰 Personal/Normal Loan Terms & Conditions

Please read all conditions carefully before proceeding with your application

✅ Eligibility Requirements
  • Minimum Enrollment Period: You must wait 4 months minimum from your account approval date before applying for any loan.
  • Account Status: Your account must be in ACTIVE status. Pending or inactive accounts cannot apply.
  • Share Amount Requirement: Your total share deduction amount must be at least ₹16,000 before you can apply for a loan.
💵 Loan Amount & Limits
  • New Members: Maximum loan amount is 5 times your total share deduction amount.
    • Example: If your share amount is ₹20,000, maximum loan = ₹1,00,000
  • Existing Members: Maximum loan amount is 10 times your total share deduction amount.
    • Example: If your share amount is ₹30,000, maximum loan = ₹3,00,000
  • Minimum Amount: Loan amount must be greater than ₹0.
  • Final Amount: The system will calculate your eligible amount considering your outstanding dues and membership type.
📋 Application Process & Approval
  • No Application Required: If your share amount ≥ ₹16,000 and it's been 12+ months since your last loan, no separate application is needed.
  • Application Required: If your share amount ≥ ₹16,000 but only 4-12 months have passed since your last loan, you need to submit a formal application.
  • Management Approval: If your share amount is below ₹16,000 or other restrictive conditions apply, you need management approval.
🔄 Loan Frequency & Timing Rules
  • First Loan: Can apply after 4 months from account approval and ₹16,000 share amount.
  • Subsequent Loans: You must wait for one of the following conditions:
    • 60 days from complete repayment of previous loan, OR
    • 4 months from the last loan approval date
    • (Whichever condition is met first)
  • Outstanding Loans: If you have any pending loan with status PENDING, CHECKED, or VERIFIED, you cannot apply for a new loan until the previous one is approved or rejected.
🚫 Restrictions & Important Notes
  • Cannot Apply If:
    • Your account status is not ACTIVE
    • You haven't completed 4 months from enrollment
    • Your share amount is below ₹16,000
    • You have a previous loan in PENDING, CHECKED, or VERIFIED status

⚠️ Important Reminder

The system will automatically verify your eligibility based on these conditions. If you don't meet any requirement, your application may be rejected or require additional approvals. Please ensure you meet all criteria before proceeding.
